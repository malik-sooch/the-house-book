Chapter Four

Project Ideas

The fun part. A few things this house is quietly asking for, whenever you are ready.

Finish the basement

It is enormous, it has daylight windows, and the renovation already gave it a clean slate. A family room, a craft room, a guest suite - it could be all three.

Deck season

The deck sits off the living room under the trees. A couple of chairs, string lights, and a container garden and it becomes the best room of the house from May to October.

Garden beds along the walk

The front and side paths are lined with planting space that is mostly mulch right now. Raised beds or perennials would change the whole approach to the house.

The garage workshop

Once the move-in boxes are gone, the garage has room for a real workbench wall. The shelves are already there.

A reading nook on the bridge

The upstairs bridge overlooks the living room and catches the best light in the house. A chair and a lamp and it is a library.

Closet built-ins

The closet drawings from the renovation show what was planned. Adding the built-ins would finish what the designers started.
